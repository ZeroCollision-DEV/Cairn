Cairn UI sounds
==================

All files in this folder (click, toggle_on, toggle_off, tab, open, close,
error) were synthesized from scratch by tools/gen_assets.py using plain sine
waves. They contain no third-party audio.

To the extent possible under law, the author(s) dedicate these sounds to the
public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. You may replace any file with your own sound; the
client embeds the versions generated by the script (src/core/SoundData.inl),
so re-run `python tools/gen_assets.py` after editing the script, or edit the
generator to load your own WAVs.
